# The specification

What the project proves, in the files a reviewer reads. The model is the random-permutation model: one uniformly random permutation `π` of the Keccak state, which the adversary queries forward and inverse, with Keccak-256 the sponge over `π` and Keccak-f[1600] `π` itself; the two statements are `KnowledgeSoundness` and `Completeness` of `Rpm.lean`. They import nothing else from the project (CI checks it); the rest is proof. The sources are leanVM's at `4af2cdddd7cd41b9df2cc7e123e314a91b0b4462` (branch `main`).

Read in this order:

1. [`Field.lean`](Field.lean): the fields `K = GF(2^64)` and `E = GF(2^192)`, and the generator `g`.
2. [`Keccak.lean`](Keccak.lean): the permutation Keccak-f[1600] (`keccakF`), Keccak-256 of bytes (Ethereum's padding, `keccak256`), a digest as four 64-bit words, the byte encodings the verifier hashes and a digest as two field elements.
3. [`Isa.lean`](Isa.lean): leanISA (programs, memory, one step, valid executions, the instructions of each opcode an execution executes, among them `KECCAK`, Keccak-f on cells) and the encoding of an instruction.
4. [`Verifier.lean`](Verifier.lean): the verifier, transcribed from leanVM's Python verifier, with the WHIR constants it reads, the Keccak GKR reduction, and the Fiat-Shamir transcript it runs on (a duplex sponge over Keccak-f[1600]); and the deferring verifier an aggregate runs on its children, which takes the value of the fixed polynomial, the bytecode, from a claim instead of computing it. The prover announces each table's row count, the table's height being the least power of two holding them; the bus discounts the rows past the count (padding rows) through a public term (`paddingWeight`, `Table.padding`). Where the pin rewrote a step of the previous pin (`3375f7a13`) without changing what it computes, the transcription keeps the earlier form and its docstring gives both the pin's lines and why the two agree: `Placement.low` (always `0`), the table sumcheck returning the column claims (`tableSumcheck`), the output lanes' claims leaving the list in `keccakPhase` rather than at `on_stack`, the weights of `_keccak_open` (`keccakSlotWeights`), and the columns `Table.padding` and `flushesKeccak` find by name rather than by index.
5. [`Extractor.lean`](Extractor.lean): the knowledge extractor, straight-line: from the proof and the adversary's Keccak-256 evaluations (a partial function from byte strings to digests; `Rpm.lean` reads them off the adversary's transcript), the announced sizes and the stack's root (read off the proof as the verifier's first two steps read them), the word the root commits to (the Merkle tree inverted through the evaluations), the candidates (the stacks whose Reed–Solomon encoding agrees with the word on at least level 0's Johnson threshold of columns), and the halting run of a candidate's memory image that is a valid execution, if there is one.
6. [`Statement.lean`](Statement.lean): a statement (a program and a public input) and the verifier on it (`verifier H F`, `verify`), its calls to Keccak-256 on byte strings and Keccak-f[1600] on states as oracle queries (`HashSpec`, `queryH`, `queryF`; this file does not say what answers them), the honest prover's type, the errors `soundnessError` and `completenessError` and the committed size of an execution (`Trace.committedSize`).
7. [`Rpm.lean`](Rpm.lean): **the statements**, in the random-permutation model, on `lean_rpm`'s lazily sampled permutation `π` of the Keccak state (not a `module`: it imports `lean_rpm`): Keccak-256 the sponge over `π`, Keccak-f[1600] `π`, the adversary querying `π` and `π⁻¹` with its transcript recorded (`rpmExperiment`); `KnowledgeSoundness` for the extractor of `Extractor.lean` on the Keccak-256 evaluations of the transcript (`extractorRpm`, `transcriptHashes`, `lean_rpm`'s `pathsOf` on the states' bytes), at `soundnessError Q + rpmError (Q + verifierPermCalls)`, and `Completeness`, at `completenessError fuel + rpmError (completenessPermCalls fuel)`.

The aggregation guest (`crates/rec_aggregation`): what a proof of it establishes, proved for the deployed guest (`LeanVM/Guest/`; see the project README):

8. The two signature schemes, leanXMSS and leanSPHINCS, imported from the projects that prove their security, here with Keccak-256 as their hash: [`lean_xmss/LeanXmss/Scheme.lean`](../../../lean_xmss/LeanXmss/Scheme.lean) and [`lean_sphincs/Sphincs/Scheme.lean`](../../../lean_sphincs/Sphincs/Scheme.lean).
9. [`Da.lean`](Da.lean): the claim on a DA root, in terms of LeanDA as [`lean_da/LeanDa/Scheme.lean`](../../../lean_da/LeanDa/Scheme.lean) defines it (the deployed commitment, the membership vector and its two-level digest), on this project's Keccak-256 and, for the challenges' sponge, Keccak-f[1600].
10. [`Node.lean`](Node.lean): a node's statement and its Keccak-256 digest, the deferred claim on the bytecode (a point and a value), the batching of the children's claims (one sumcheck over the duplex sponge), `NodeRel` and `NodeSpec` (what a node establishes, with a witness and with some witness) and the outer verifier.
11. [`Guest.lean`](Guest.lean): a decoder (a seed, a statement and a witness read off a memory), and what a valid execution of the guest establishes with it: `GuestKnowledge` (every valid execution decodes to a statement of its input's digest and a witness holding it; no oracle); the Keccak-256 collision candidates of two statements' digests and `Explains`.
12. [`Aggregate.lean`](Aggregate.lean): `AggregateSound`, what the outer verifier's acceptance of a proof of the guest establishes in the random-permutation model of `Rpm.lean` (the headline for a consumer): except with the bound of `KnowledgeSoundness`, the execution `extractorRpm` reads off the proof and the adversary's transcript explains the accepted statement (not a `module`: it imports `Rpm.lean`).

The same statements with the hash, the permutation and the `KECCAK` relation as parameters, each recovering the deployed one (bridge lemmas `…_deployed`, `…_id`), for proofs about the guest:

13. [`IsaWith.lean`](IsaWith.lean): leanISA with the relation `KECCAK` checks a parameter (`KeccakSem`): the deployed one (`keccakDeployed`, Keccak-f[1600] on the cells) and the reduction-blind one (`keccakFree`, canonical cells).
14. [`NodeWith.lean`](NodeWith.lean) and [`DaWith.lean`](DaWith.lean): the node's digests, signature verification, batching (from a sponge state) and DA claim with the hash and the permutation in any monad, and `NodeRelWith`, `Witness.OpensWith` over an answered (`Option`-valued) hash and permutation.

[`Fast/`](Fast/) needs no review: it holds `@[csimp]` theorems, each proved, that make the compiled field arithmetic, Keccak-f[1600] and Keccak-256, LeanDA's membership vector and the signature schemes' verifiers fast. They change no definition.

The proofs are `LeanVM.knowledgeSoundness_holds` and `LeanVM.completeness_holds` in [`LeanVM/Protocol/LeanVMRpm.lean`](../Protocol/LeanVMRpm.lean), and `LeanVM.Guest.End.aggregate_sound_outer : AggregateSound guest decode` in [`LeanVM/Guest/EndRpm.lean`](../Guest/EndRpm.lean). They go through the two-oracle random-oracle model (Keccak-256 and Keccak-f[1600] independent random oracles), which is not part of the specification: it is stated in [`LeanVM/Protocol/Rom/Statement.lean`](../Protocol/Rom/Statement.lean) and proved in [`LeanVMSound.lean`](../Protocol/LeanVMSound.lean) and [`LeanVM.lean`](../Protocol/LeanVM.lean), and `lean_rpm`'s indifferentiability of the sponge (`LeanRpm.keccak256_transfer2`, `q² / 2^508`) moves it to the random-permutation model.
