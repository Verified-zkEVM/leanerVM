\chapter[ArkLib: exists, pending, ours]{ArkLib: what exists, what is pending, what is ours}\label{ch:arklib-landscape}

The blueprint plans to write several generic components locally and to consume others from
ArkLib at a later pin. Much of the sumcheck, the composition and the GKR work has moved
upstream since the blueprint was written, at revisions later than either pin and in open pull
requests. This chapter says, component by component, what ArkLib implements at the new pin
(\code{7653a901}), what has landed on its \code{main} since, what its open pull requests would
supply and how close they are, what the blueprint plans locally that already exists upstream,
and what remains leanerVM's own because nothing upstream is planned for leanVM's specific
shapes. It ends with a revised upstream ledger for the blueprint. The survey was made by a
review agent from ArkLib's history and pull requests; its Markdown form is
\file{dossiers/arklib-landscape.md} on the review branch.
